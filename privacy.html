Privacy Policy

SuiteWash and Fold LLC  ·  Last updated: June 2026

SuiteWash and Fold LLC ("SuiteWash," "we," "our," or "us") operates suitewashandfold.com and provides laundry subscription services to apartment residents in the Detroit metro area. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, subscribe to a plan, or contact us:

  • Name, email address, and password
  • Phone number, building name, unit number, and city
  • Wash preferences, scent preferences, and special instructions
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we never store card numbers)
  • Pickup history and service usage

We also collect limited technical data automatically, including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, via standard web server logs and Cloudflare infrastructure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our laundry pickup and delivery service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To communicate with you about pickups, service updates, and account activity
  • To fulfill your wash preferences and special instructions on each order
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing and subscription management
  • Supabase — for secure database storage of your account and pickup data
  • Resend — for transactional email delivery (confirmations, password resets)
  • Your property management company — only to coordinate building access for pickups, limited to your name and unit number

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), row-level security on our database so residents can only access their own data, and no storage of payment card details. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription and request deletion of your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the email below. Michigan residents have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws.

7. Cookies

We use only essential session cookies necessary for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
