Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SuiteWash and Fold LLC ("SuiteWash," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, email, phone number, password
Service informationAddress, ZIP code, building/unit, wash and scent preferences, special instructions
Payment informationProcessed and stored directly by our payment processor, Stripe — we do not store full card numbers
Usage informationPickup history, plan usage, add-on purchases, referral activity
Technical informationDevice and browser type, IP address, general analytics via Google Analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and fulfill pickups and deliveries
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To communicate with you about your orders, account, or service updates
  • To improve our service and understand how residents use SuiteWash
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of our referral program

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Payment processing: Stripe, Inc. processes all payments on our behalf and maintains its own privacy practices.
  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database and authentication) and Cloudflare (hosting) store and transmit account and service data on our behalf.
  • Service delivery: Our laundry facility partners receive only the information needed to fulfill your order (such as wash preferences), not your full account details.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SuiteWash, our customers, or others.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

5. Your Choices

  • You can update your account information and preferences anytime from your dashboard.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time; this stops future billing but does not automatically delete your historical order data.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by emailing hello@suitewashandfold.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication, and PCI-compliant payment processing through Stripe. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

SuiteWash is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to hello@suitewashandfold.com.
