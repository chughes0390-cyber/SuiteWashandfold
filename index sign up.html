The Process

Simple as leaving a bag at your door.

01
Schedule a Pickup

Choose your preferred pickup day via the resident app. We sync with your building's schedule.

02
Leave It at Your Door

Bag your laundry and leave it outside by 8am on pickup day. That's it — no trips downstairs.

03
We Handle Everything

Your clothes are washed, dried, and folded by our team at a nearby facility using your preferences.

04
Delivered Back Clean

Within 48 hours, your fresh, folded laundry is delivered back to your door. Repeat monthly.
