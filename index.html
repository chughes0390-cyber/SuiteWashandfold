Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Leave your bag outside your door before 8 AM. We collect, wash, fold, and return within 48 hours. No interaction needed.
Add instructions during signup (e.g. "air dry delicates"). We follow your preferences every cycle. Update anytime from your dashboard.
Cancel anytime from your dashboard or through the Stripe billing portal. No fees, no lock-in. Billed monthly.
Premium hypoallergenic detergent as standard. Add preferences in your profile. Subscribe to the Detergent Bundle add-on to supply your own pods.
We treat every garment with care. Our Garment Protection add-on covers up to $150 per incident. Email hello@suitewashandfold.com and we'll make it right.
SuiteWash serves select Detroit metro apartment buildings. Your building needs to be a registered partner. Click "Check My Building" or ask your property manager.
