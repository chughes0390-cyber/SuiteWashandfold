The Process

Up and running in 48 hours.

01
Sign the Partner Agreement

A simple one-page agreement. No exclusivity required. We handle all resident-facing terms and service agreements separately.

02

SuiteWash handles all resident outreach, signups, and app onboarding. You provide building access — we do the rest.
